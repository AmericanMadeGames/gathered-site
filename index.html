Gathered

Openers, games, study notes, and prayer for church small groups, all in one place.

Gathered helps church small group leaders spend less time preparing and more time with their people. It brings icebreaker questions, Bible games, study notes, and prayer requests into one simple app on the leader's phone.

  • Free forever
  • No ads
  • Works offline
  • Collects no data

Shaped around your meeting

Set up your group once, and Gathered walks with you through the whole meeting.

  1. 1

    Open with a question

    Hundreds of icebreaker questions, from light to deep, chosen to fit your group. Questions you've already used don't come back around, and seasonal packs appear for Christmas and Easter.

  2. 2

    Play a game

    Seven games that run on one phone, including This or That, Who in the Bible Am I?, Verse Scramble, Bible Emoji Story, Who Said It?, Books of the Bible, and Two Truths and a Lie.

  3. 3

    Study

    Keep the week's passage and notes in one place, with past notes saved by meeting date.

  4. 4

    Close in prayer

    Write down requests, keep private ones private, read through them during a closing prayer, and copy a list of shared requests to send to the group.

Keeps time so you don't have to

Gathered knows when your group meets, counts your meetings, and handles skipped weeks and breaks. A quiet timer keeps the opener and study on track, and it vibrates instead of chiming, so a good conversation never gets cut off.

Private by design

Gathered does not collect any data, now or ever. Your groups, study notes, and prayer requests stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and Gathered never connects to a server. Read the full privacy policy.
