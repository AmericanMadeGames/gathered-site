Common questions

When can I get Gathered?

The iOS beta is open now. Join it on TestFlight, or see "Join the beta" above for the steps. Gathered will come to the App Store after testing.

Who is Gathered for?

Leaders of church small groups: youth groups, young adults, Bible studies, couples, men's and women's groups, seniors, and newcomers. A leader sets up their group once, and Gathered shapes the whole meeting around it.

Is it really free?

Yes, free now and forever, with no ads. Gathered was made as a service to the church, the body of Christ, just as salvation is a free gift.

"For it is by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God." Ephesians 2:8 (BSB)

Does everyone in the group need the app?

No. Gathered is made for the leader's phone. The games run on one phone, so the group can play together without downloading anything.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Gathered works offline and never connects to a server.

Who can see our prayer requests?

Only the leader, on their own phone. Private requests stay private. A list of shared requests leaves the phone only when the leader copies it and chooses to send it to the group.

What if our group skips a week or takes a break?

Gathered knows when your group meets, counts meetings, and handles skipped weeks and breaks.

Will the timer interrupt a good conversation?

No. The timer for the opener and study vibrates quietly instead of chiming, so it keeps you on track without cutting anyone off.

What happens if I delete the app or get a new phone?

Everything in Gathered lives only on your phone and is never sent to us, so we have no copy and can't restore it for you.
