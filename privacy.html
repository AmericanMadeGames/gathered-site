Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Gathered does not collect any data, now or ever.

Your information stays on your phone

Everything a leader enters into Gathered, including groups, study notes, and prayer requests, stays on their phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What Gathered doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No connection to any server

Prayer requests

Private requests stay private on the leader's phone. A prayer list leaves the phone only when the leader copies one and chooses to send it, for example in a text to the group. Once sent, that message is handled by whatever app the leader used to send it.

Children and youth groups

Gathered can be used by leaders of groups of any age, including youth groups. Because Gathered collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you email us

If you write to us at developer@americanmadegames.com, we'll see your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply to you, and we never share it or add you to a mailing list.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
